Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Locked Inn · Last updated: 02/08/2026
Welcome to Locked Inn. This page explains, in plain language, what information we collect, how we use it, and the rules for using the app. By creating an account, you agree to what's written here. If you're under 18, please read this with a parent or guardian.

Part 1 — Privacy Policy

Who we are

Locked Inn is a free study app for students, accessible at locked-inn1.netlify.app. This policy covers the information handled when you use it.

What we collect

We only collect what's needed to run the app:

  • Your email address and first name — used to create and identify your account.
  • Your study content — the flashcards, notes, to-do items, exam reminders, study PDFs, and tutor chats you create. These are saved so you can access them across your devices.
  • A securely scrambled version of your password — we never see or store your actual password. It is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos (beyond an image you choose to send to the AI tutor in the moment), or any payment information.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. Ever.
  • We do not show third-party advertising inside the app.

Where your information is stored

Your account and study data are stored securely using Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore), a widely used and trusted service. Security rules ensure that only you can access your own data. Photos you send to the AI tutor are processed to generate a response and are not permanently saved.

How we use your information

Only to make the app work for you — to log you in, save your progress, sync it across devices, send you exam reminders, and generate AI responses and study material you request. That's it.

Third-party services we rely on

  • Google Firebase — for sign-in and storing your data.
  • Google Gemini (AI) — the text and images you send to the AI tutor or study generator are processed by Google's AI service to create a response. Don't send sensitive personal information through the tutor.
  • Netlify — hosts the app.

Your rights

  • You can view and edit your study content any time inside the app.
  • You can delete individual items (notes, decks, PDFs) yourself.
  • You can request that your entire account and its data be deleted — contact us at multi2editor@gmail.com and we'll remove it.

Children and minors

Locked Inn is designed for students, including those under 18. We collect as little information as possible. If you are under 18, please use the app with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. If a parent or guardian wants a child's account removed, contact us at multi2editor@gmail.com.

A note on POPIA

We aim to handle your information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA): we collect only what's necessary, use it only for the stated purpose, keep it secure, and let you request its deletion.
